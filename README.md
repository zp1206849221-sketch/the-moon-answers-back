Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Moon Finally Answers Back**: one line,
written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`, in the voice of the Moon
replying at last to everyone who has ever stared up at it.

> Four billion years of wishes, werewolf howls, and 3 a.m. promises nobody
> kept, and not one of you down there has ever asked how I'm doing. I'm fine.
> I'm a rock. But still.

**The joke:** for four billion years the Moon has been humanity's unpaid
confessional — wished on, howled at, and sworn eternal love to at 3 a.m. —
and its first-ever reply isn't anger, it's the serene, slightly wounded
dignity of someone who has heard everything and been asked nothing. The
comeback lands in the deadpan tail: "I'm fine. I'm a rock. But still."

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py                          # writes /output/entry.txt
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
